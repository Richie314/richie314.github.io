Hello world, I'm Riccardo. I'm from Livorno and live in Pisa, where I study computer science at the [University of Pisa](https://www.unipi.it) and attend the [Sant'Anna School of Advanced Studies](https://santannapisa.it).

I like writing software, running my own services, and tinkering with electronics. Most of my projects involve PHP, JavaScript, Python, C#, or Docker. You can find them on [GitHub](https://github.com/Richie314).

## Embedded systems

I started using Arduinos when I was in high school and later moved on to ESP32 boards and Internet of Things.
I enjoy creating tools that interact with the real world and keep a handful of side projects for my free time. 

## Cybersecurity

I got into cybersecurity and CTFs during my first year at university. I'm part of [BeautifulSSSUP](https://ctftime.org/team/177099), and I mainly work on web challenges, reverse engineering, and OSINT. 

At my very first real CTF competition, my team (University of Pisa) came third nationwide - a result that only deepened my interest in the field.
