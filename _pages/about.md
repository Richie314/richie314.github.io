Hi, I'm Riccardo. I'm from Livorno and live in Pisa, where I study computer science at the [University of Pisa](https://www.unipi.it) and attend the [Sant'Anna School of Advanced Studies](https://santannapisa.it).

I like writing software, running my own services, and tinkering with electronics. Most of my projects involve PHP, JavaScript, Python, C#, or Docker. You can find them on [GitHub](https://github.com/Richie314).

## Cybersecurity

I got into cybersecurity and CTFs during my first year at university. I'm part of [BeautifulSSSUP](https://ctftime.org/team/177099), and I mainly work on web challenges, reverse engineering, and OSINT. My University of Pisa team came third nationally at CyberChallenge.

## Outside university

I used to compete in the triple jump and long jump, and I still follow athletics. My results are on [Atletica.me](https://atletica.me/atleta/Riccardo-Ciucci/335769). I've also been a LEGO fan since childhood.
